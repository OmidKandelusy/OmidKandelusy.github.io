I am an Electrical Engineering PhD graduate; my education were mostly focused on theoretical analysis of the physical layer in wireless communication systems, and investigation of communication protocols. I have experience in designing and implementing the communication protocols for wireless sensor networks for 2.4 GHz as well as sub GHz ISM bands. I was a postdoctoral researcher and lecturer at the University of Kansas and I have research experience in dynamic spectrum access, signal processing, coding theory, and federated machine learning. I have a decent software development experience, particularly in embedded context and real time operating systems (RTOS).

I am from Iran where I received my BSc and MSc in electrical engineering. I came to the US to pursue my PhD reseasrch journey in 2017 and I graduated from University of New Hampshire in 2021. After my PhD graduation, I moved to Kansas to work as a postdoctoral researcher and lecturer at the University of Kansas. I am currently a wireless communication engineer at Passivelogic's sensor group where we are developing our wireless sensor network for our AI-driven digital twin; our inteligent control solution would transform the efficiency of the future buildings and cities.
